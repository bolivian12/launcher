Provider logos

These logos identify the modpack and mod services EBALIA can browse and import from:
ATLauncher, CurseForge, Feed The Beast (FTB and FTB Legacy), Modrinth and Technic.
They are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify each service.

The images were rasterized to 96 px from the copies distributed with Prism Launcher
(https://github.com/PrismLauncher/PrismLauncher, launcher/resources/multimc,
commit 323609694d04a4715e88a087ccf7de05696c998c).
